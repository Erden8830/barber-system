# Barber Pro Demo Script — 5 minutes

## Setup (before you walk in)
1. Open your phone browser to barberpro-mn.duckdns.org
2. Have kingsbarber demo shop loaded and logged in
3. Open two tabs: one for customer queue, one for admin panel

---

## Minute 1: "This is your customer's experience"
**You say:** "A customer walks up to your shop, sees a QR code on the counter."

**You do:** Show the queue join page (barberpro-mn.duckdns.org/shop/kingsbarber)
- Point at the QR code on the page
- "They scan, pick their barber, enter their phone number. Done."
- Actually join the queue with a dummy phone number
- Show the position number and estimated wait
- "They know exactly where they are. No hovering around asking 'is it my turn yet?'"

## Minute 2: "Your barber taps one button"
**You say:** "Your barber finishes a haircut, opens this page."

**You do:** Switch to admin page → Queue tab
- Show the queue list with the dummy customer you just added
- Click "Дараагийнх" (Call Next)
- "One tap. The next customer's phone buzzes. That's it."
- "Barbers don't need training. They've been calling names for years — now they tap instead."

## Minute 3: "Customers book while you sleep"
**You say:** "It's 11pm. You're home. A customer remembers they need a haircut tomorrow."

**You do:** Open the booking page (barberpro-mn.duckdns.org/shop/kingsbarber → booking)
- Pick a barber, pick a service, pick a time slot
- "Two taps. They're booked. You wake up to a full schedule."
- "Your phone didn't ring at 11pm. You didn't DM back and forth on Instagram."

## Minute 4: "Know who's actually working"
**You say:** "You have 3 barbers. Who made the most this month?"

**You do:** Admin panel → Analytics / Revenue
- Show per-barber breakdown if available, or general stats
- "Commission auto-calculated. No notebook, no 'I think I did 12 haircuts today.'"
- "And you can see who hasn't shown up. That's money walking out the door."

## Minute 5: Close + pricing
**You say:** "₮99,000 a month. First month free. That's about 1.5 haircuts."

**Pause. Let them process.**

**If they hesitate:**
- "Your walk-ins alone — even if you save ONE customer per day who would've left, that's ₮30,000 a day. ₮900,000 a month."
- "Free for a month. If it doesn't make you money, cancel. No contract."

**If they say yes:**
- Open signup page on their phone
- barberpro-mn.duckdns.org/signup
- Help them pick their shop slug, set password
- Show them where to print the QR code

---

## Objection cheat sheet
| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Too expensive" | "1.5 haircuts covers it. If it saves 2 no-shows a month, it paid for itself." |
| "My barbers are old school" | "They tap one button. Same as calling a name. Easier actually." |
| "We use Instagram DMs" | "Instagram can't tell you who booked, who showed up, or who hasn't come back in 30 days." |
| "barber.mn is cheaper" | "They're booking only. No queue for walk-ins, no commission tracking, no SMS. You'll outgrow them." |
| "I'll think about it" | "Free first month. Try it with one barber. If it doesn't help, you lost nothing." |

## What NOT to say
- Don't mention "SMS" — not ready yet
- Don't mention bugs or things you're still building
- Don't mention competitors by name unless they bring them up
- Don't oversell — "it does everything" sounds fake
