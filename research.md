## 1. ONLINE BOOKING — Lost revenue you're recovering

| Stat | Source |
|------|--------|
| **46-50%** of bookings happen outside business hours | SimpleSalon |
| **94%** of clients prefer providers with online booking | SimpleSalon |
| **42%** of potential clients won't call back if there's no online booking | SimpleSalon |
| **49% lower no-show rate** for online bookings vs phone bookings | SimpleSalon |
| Automated reminders reduce no-shows by **up to 40%** | SimpleSalon |
| Average salon loses **$2,500-$5,000/month** (~₮8-16M) to no-shows | SimpleSalon |

**Mongolian math:**
A Ulaanbaatar barbershop doing 15 cuts/day at ₮35,000 average:
- 2 no-shows/day = ₮2,100,000/month lost
- Reduce by 40% = ₮840,000 recovered/month
- System pays for itself in 4 days
